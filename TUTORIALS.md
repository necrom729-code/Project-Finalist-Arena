# Finalist Arena - Tutorials for Beginners

## Tutorial 1: Movement & Needs
- Walk, run, climb trees, climb rock walls, swim
- Monitor Health, Stamina, Hygiene, Sleep meters
- Critical needs reduce performance; critical health = audio distortion

## Tutorial 2: Crafting Basics
- Gather materials (wood, stone, fiber, hide)
- Open crafting menu, select recipe, craft item
- Manual books unlock new recipes
- Tech tier (from kill points) gates advanced crafting

## Tutorial 3: Combat
- Melee and ranged weapons have execution animations
- Aim for weak spots (highlighted with Archery skill)
- Proximity voice chat: hear teammates and nearby neutral players
- Teams up to 8; betrayal allowed; only 1 winner

## Tutorial 4: The Zone
- Zone shrinks differently per biome (fire, earthquake, ice break, meteor, sandstorm)
- Outside zone = escalating damage until death
- Materials and living beings in zone are destroyed

## Tutorial 5: AI Threats
- Mutated animals and mutated humans hunt players who stay too long
- Toxic waste undead spawn in hazardous areas
- Death by predator triggers unique death camera showing predator's victory

## Tutorial 6: Event Waves
- Every 10 minutes, a unique danger activates (Blood Tsunami, Acid Rain, etc.)
- Avoid or defend; failure = heavy damage or death

## Tutorial 7: Kill Points & Crates
- Each kill = bonus points
- Spend points at supply crates based on tech tier
- Streamers: chat hype (YouTube/Twitch/TikTok/Facebook) = "Arena Hyped" bonus supplies

## Tutorial 8: Winning
- Last person standing wins
- Gamemaker cutscene declares winner; spectators cheer
