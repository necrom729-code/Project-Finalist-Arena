# Finalist Arena - Death Camera, Gore & Audio

## Death Camera
- When a player is killed by AI (animal, mutated, undead), camera switches to predator's perspective
- Predator performs a victory animation/eating pose
- Camera lingers for 3-5 seconds before spectating or respawn lobby

## Gore & Visceral Effects
- Blood decals, splatters, pools on impact
- Dismemberment on melee executions (weapon-specific)
- Wound system: bleeding, limb damage, visible injuries
- Niagara-based blood particles and gore gibs

## Audio Design
- Heavy visceral impact sounds (bone crunch, flesh tear, weapon strikes)
- Weapon-specific impact foley
- Dynamic combat layers amplify tension during fights
- Music distorts when player health is critical (<20 HP): bitcrush, lowpass, heartbeat pulse
- Spectator crowd noise scales with streamer chat hype
