# Finalist Arena - Main Menu Music Concept

## Title: "The Spectator's Glade"

**Tone:** Post-apocalyptic, suspenseful, sadistic spectacle, horror survival

**Structure:**
1. **Intro (0:00-0:45):** Distant crowd murmuring, low cello drone, sparse piano notes, echoing stadium ambience
2. **Build (0:45-1:45):** Tense strings rise, percussion enters (distant war drums), spectator cheers swell like waves
3. **Climax (1:45-2:30):** Full orchestral horror swell, choir of spectators chanting, metallic percussion, distorted bass
4. **Outro (2:30-3:00):** Fade to distant cheering, heartbeat pulse, single piano note

**Dynamic Audio Layers:**
- **Combat Layer:** Stinger strings + brass hits layer on top during intense fights
- **Critical Health Layer:** When player HP < 20%, music distorts (bitcrush + lowpass filter + heartbeat)
- **Hype Layer:** Spectator volume increases with streamer chat hype (YouTube/Twitch/TikTok/Facebook)

**Lyrics (whispered, distant):**
_"One remains. The arena watches. The elite feast on your survival."_

**Implementation:** UE5 MetaSounds with runtime DSP (distortion filter on critical health).
