# Finalist Arena - 20 Event Waves (Every 10 Minutes)

Each wave is biome-specific and escalates in intensity. Players must avoid or defend.

| Wave | Name | Description | Biome |
|------|------|-------------|-------|
| 1 | Blood Tsunami | Huge wave of blood-filled water with carnivore zombie-fish humans (fins, echolocation) | All (water zones) |
| 2 | Acid Rain | Corrosive rain damages HP and degrades equipment | Forest, Volcano |
| 3 | Lightning Storm | Random lightning strikes ignite trees and deal shock damage | Forest, Safari |
| 4 | Mutated Wasp Swarm | Fast aerial AI that poisons on contact | Forest, Safari |
| 5 | Volcanic Ash Cloud | Suffocation hazard, visibility reduced | Volcano |
| 6 | Quicksand Pit Collapse | Ground liquefies, swallowing players and loot | Safari, Desert |
| 7 | Arctic Blizzard | Zero visibility, rapid Sleep/Hygiene drain | Arctic |
| 8 | Meteor Shower | Burning meteors impact, creating fire zones | Volcano, Desert |
| 9 | Toxic Gas Vent | Poison fog erupts from ground; HP drains rapidly | Unique, Volcano |
| 10 | Predator Pack Alpha | Alpha mutated beast leads pack hunt | Safari, Forest |
| 11 | Tree Collapse | Massive trees fall, crushing players underneath | Forest |
| 12 | Fissure Opening | Earthquake cracks open the earth | Safari |
| 13 | Ice Shelf Calving | Ice breaks apart, players fall into freezing water | Arctic |
| 14 | Lava Flow Surge | Lava rivers advance across the map | Volcano |
| 15 | Sandstorm Wall | Blinding sand, navigation disabled | Desert |
| 16 | Mutated Plant Bloom | Spore explosion causes hallucination/paranoia | Forest, Unique |
| 17 | Radiation Pulse | Periodic radiation ticks; HP and gear degrade | Unique, Volcano |
| 18 | Blood Moon | All AI aggression doubled; night vision disabled | All |
| 19 | Echo Scream | Audio hallucinations; players hear false footsteps/gunfire | All |
| 20 | Final Circle Collapse | Zone collapses to a single point; last fight | All |
